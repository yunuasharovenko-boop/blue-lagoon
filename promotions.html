Bonus terms in plain words

What is a wagering requirement?

It is how much you must bet before bonus money becomes withdrawable cash. "35×" on a $100 bonus means $3,500 in total bets.

Can I withdraw a bonus straight away?

No. Bonus funds must be wagered first. Your own deposited money can be withdrawn at any time, but doing so cancels the active bonus.

Do bonuses expire?

Yes — most are valid for 30 days, free spins for 7 days. Anything unused after that is removed automatically.

Is there a maximum bet while wagering?

Yes, $5 per spin or hand. Bets above that may void the bonus, so keep stakes low until wagering is complete.
