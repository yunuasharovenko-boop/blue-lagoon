Signs it may be a problem

If you answer yes to any of these, please consider using the tools above or contacting one of the organisations below.

  • Do you gamble to escape stress, boredom or low mood?
  • Have you tried to win back money you lost ("chasing losses")?
  • Do you spend more money or time than you planned?
  • Have you borrowed money or sold anything to gamble?
  • Have you hidden your gambling from family or friends?
  • Has gambling affected your work, studies or relationships?
